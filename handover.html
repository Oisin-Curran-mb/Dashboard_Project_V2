V1 → V2 Handover — Shelby Financials Dashboard

Purpose of this doc: to tell developers what changed between V1 (Phase 1 branch/design-sandbox/Widget Container Demo/index.html) and V2 (Complete Version 2/index.html), widget by widget, in plain English — no code. Every widget is tagged with an overall change type so you know how much attention it needs before you start:

  • Visual Only — appearance changed, nothing else.
  • New Feature or Logic — new capability, or a calculation/behavior change, on top of the same basic design.
  • Complete Change — the widget was substantially or fully rebuilt.
  • New Widget — did not exist in V1 (Financial KPI and Alerts & Actions).

This document covers every widget in the Version 2 build: the seventeen carried over from V1, with V1 and V2 screenshots side by side, what changed and where, and developer notes on any logic; plus the two new in V2, Financial KPI, with Aditya's design spec linked from its section, and Alerts & Actions, not yet written up.

Developer Notes

Sparkline logic

The sparkline works the same way as W07 Deposits on Hand's Glance sparkline. Same hover text and the same rule for picking the interval. One difference in look: W01 shows the line only, with no shading underneath (set with the --spark-area-opacity token, 0 for W01). Build and test the two together.

  • It plots the Actual for each posted period in the selected time window. Periods that haven't posted yet are left out.
  • The interval follows the Deposits rule: the smallest step that still reads as a trend.
    • This month: by week (Deposits "month" = weekly points)
    • This period: by week
    • This quarter: by month (Deposits "quarter" = monthly points)
    • This year (last 12 months): by month (Deposits "year" = monthly points)
    • This fiscal year: by month (Deposits "fiscal year" = monthly points)
  • It needs at least 2 posted points. With fewer, the sparkline is hidden.
  • It follows the account filter (Income accounts, Expense accounts or Special report).
  • Hover text works like Deposits: it shows the period label (e.g. "Mar") and its amount.
  • It uses the same sparkline drawing as Deposits, so a change to one changes both.
  • One difference from Deposits: Deposits always shows a fixed run of 8 to 12 points ending today, even when that reaches back before the chosen window. Budget only plots periods inside the chosen window, because it compares against that window's budget.

Percentage logic

  • % = Variance ÷ Budget × 100, shown without a sign and rounded to 1 decimal place.
  • Budget and Actual are the totals of the posted periods only in the selected time window, the same totals as the big number ("Total, posted so far").
  • Variance for income accounts = Actual − Budget. For expense accounts it's Budget − Actual. Either way, a positive variance is favourable.
  • The +/− sign and the Favourable / Unfavourable pill show the direction; the % only shows the size.
  • If no budget is set up, don't show the % at all, on any size or tab. The same goes for a time window whose posted budget adds up to $0. Never divide by zero, and never show "0%" or "100%" in its place.
  • It's the same % on every size and tab. It changes with the account filter and time window, but not with the D / W / M / P / Q / Y toggle.
  • Example (This fiscal year, Jan–Jul): Budget $312,000, Actual $317,900, so Variance = +$5,900 and 5,900 ÷ 312,000 = 1.89%, shown as 1.9%.

"This period" length

  • A fiscal period's length is set by each organization, from one month up to three. Don't assume it's one month.
  • The demo treats This period as one month (Jul 1–Jul 31, 2026), so it shows the same figures as This month. The real build must take the current period's start and end dates from the organization's fiscal calendar.
  • Check the interval toggle against the real period length. The rule is that an interval is offered only when it gives 2 to 31 points. A one-month period offers D, W, M; a three-month period gives about 90 days, so D must be greyed out and it offers W, M.

  • Pop-up - Distribution detail, Employee Break Down: every employee paid from that distribution, with their total pay and a total for all employees.
  • Pop-up - Distribution detail, Employee Break Down: sorted by Employee name A–Z by default. Click Total pay to sort by pay, largest first.

Developer Notes

Filter logic

  • Period, Distribution and Pay type are three separate filters. All three apply together: the total, table, chart and Glance mix bar only count pay that matches every selected filter.
  • Distribution offers All distributions or one distribution. Pay type offers All pay types or one pay type.
  • Changing a filter inside the pop-up only changes the pop-up. The card behind it keeps its own filters.

Pop-up sorting

  • Top table: always Amount, largest first. No user sorting.
  • Employee Break Down: Employee name A–Z by default, every time the pop-up opens. Clicking Employee flips A–Z / Z–A. Clicking Total pay sorts largest first, and a second click smallest first. Ties fall back to name A–Z.
  • Expanded employee: pay types are always largest first. The total row is the sum of that employee's pay types under the current filters.

Export to Excel

  • Export to Excel in the distribution pop-up downloads everything on screen, based on the pop-up's filters (the period, distribution and pay type chips):
    • 1. Header total: the distribution's total for the selected filters (e.g. $24,593).
    • 2. Pay types: the pay type table, one row each, with Pay type, % of total and Amount, plus the Total row.
    • 3. Employee Break Down: every employee paid from that distribution under the same filters, one row each, with Employee and Total pay, plus the total for all employees.
  • It does not download the child items: the pay type split you see when an employee is expanded is not in the file.
  • The employee rows are personal pay data, so the export should follow the same permission as the Employee Break Down API.

Data needed

  • The Employee Break Down needs pay per employee, per distribution, per pay type, for the selected period. V1 only needed totals per distribution and pay type, so this is new data the backend has to supply.
  • The Employee Break Down must come from its own, separate API call. Don't add employee details to the existing distribution / pay type response. The pop-up may be permission gated, and hiding data in the screen doesn't protect it: anything in an API response can be read from the browser, even if it's never shown. Only call the employee API when the pop-up opens, and only return data if the user has permission to see employee pay. The card and the pop-up's top table keep using the totals API, which has no employee names or amounts.
  • Only employees with pay above $0 under the current filters are listed. If none, the list shows "No employees paid in this period".

Instalment schedule

  • Pop-up - Pledge expanded: new. Click a pledge to open its instalment schedule straight under the row. The pledge's name, term and status are already on the row above, so there is no extra header line. The row and its schedule share a light grey bar down the left, with the schedule indented under the name, so they read as one block.
  • Pop-up - Pledge expanded: a table of Due date, Amount due, Paid and Status. The first row under the headings is the total ("Total, 24 instalments", with the pledged amount and the amount paid), then every instalment below it with its status (Paid, Part paid, In grace period, Missed, Not due yet).

Developer Notes

Card logic is unchanged

  • The card (Glance, Explore, Detail, the pace cards, the tables and the Progress bars) works exactly as in V1: each activity is paced evenly over its own term. Expected = pledge × (days elapsed ÷ days in the term). Behind, On track and Ahead come from that.
  • The one exception is the Pledges behind column, which counts pledges using the pop-up's instalment logic (below). Every money figure on the card stays on the V1 basis.

Pop-up logic is new

  • The pop-up works from every individual pledge under the activity, each with its own begin date, end date and instalment schedule. A pledge is checked against the instalments that have actually fallen due, not just how much money has come in. This shows what is truly owed so far and whether a pledge is really behind.
  • Payments are applied to the oldest instalment first, so a gap always shows as the earliest unpaid instalment.
  • Behind: an instalment has been unpaid for more than 7 days after its due date.
  • In grace period: an unpaid instalment is still inside that 7-day grace period. It doesn't count as behind. Amount in grace period = the unpaid part of those instalments.
  • Amount behind = for each pledge, expected so far − paid − amount in grace period, never below $0, then added up. "Expected so far" is every instalment whose due date has passed. A pledge paid ahead counts as $0, so it can't hide one that is behind.

Why the pop-up's numbers can differ from the card

  • Pledged, Paid, Outstanding and % Paid always match the card.
  • Behind can differ. The card's Behind / On track / Ahead paces the activity evenly over its term. The pop-up's Pledges behind counts pledges with an instalment more than 7 days late. This is intended: the pop-up is the more accurate view of who has actually missed a payment.
  • The pop-up shows no expected figure. Money that is due but still inside its grace period is shown on its own as Amount in grace period.
  • The pop-up explains this behind the ⓘ icon in its header.

Export to Excel

  • Export to Excel in the pledges pop-up downloads a file with two sections:
    • 1. Header totals: the summary figures at the top of the pop-up: Total pledged, Paid to date, Outstanding, % Paid, Pledges behind, Amount behind, Pledges in grace period and Amount in grace period.
    • 2. List of pledges: every pledge under the activity, one row each, with the same columns as the pop-up: Name, Begin date, End date, Pledged amount, Paid, Outstanding and Status. In this example that is all 60 pledges, not just the ones loaded on screen.
  • It does not download the child items: the instalment schedule rows you see when a pledge is expanded (Due date, Amount due, Paid, Status) are not in the file.

Loading

  • The pledge list loads 20 pledges at a time. "Load more" adds the next 20; a sort change goes back to the first 20. The backend should support fetching the list in batches (offset and count) for activities with many pledges.

Data needed

  • Every pledge's begin date, end date, pledged amount, payment frequency and receipts, so the instalment schedule can be built. V1 only needed activity totals and receipts, so this is new data the backend has to supply.

Overview

  1. Six aging bands instead of five. "Current" now means not yet due, and a new "1-30 days" band holds invoices up to a month late.

  4. A tidier pop-up. Search by customer or invoice number, sortable column headers, a full-width list, and an expanded invoice with the customer's contact details and the invoice's Details / Attachments / Note.

What's Different

Aging bands

  • Explore - Aging: six rows: Current ("not yet due"), 1-30 days ("under 1 month over"), 31-60, 61-90, 91-120 and 121+ days, each with a short description under the name. V1's "Current" ("not yet overdue") also held invoices up to 30 days past due.

  • Glance: the mix bar has six segments to match.

Overdue badge

  • Glance, Explore - Aging, Detail - Aging: "$X overdue (Y%)" now counts every invoice past its due date (e.g. $78,390 of $84,570, 93%). V1 counted only invoices more than 30 days past due.

Caption

  • Explore - Aging, Detail - Aging: the "Owed to you, oldest balances first." line under the total is gone.

Table and Pie views

  • Explore - Aging: the bars are now a table: Aging, Invoices and Amount, every heading sortable (default: Aging, Current first). Each band has its colour dot, and a "Total outstanding" row closes the table.
  • Explore - Aging: new Table / Pie switch, under Aging / Customers and level with the total.
  • Explore - Customers: the bars are now a table: Customer (with its invoice count underneath), Aging (the age of the customer's oldest invoice, e.g. "145d", or "Current") and Balance, every heading sortable (default: Balance, highest first). The dot shows the customer's most overdue band.
  • Explore - Aging Pie, Explore - Customers Pie: a donut of each band's or customer's share, with a legend beside it giving amount and %. The customer legend scrolls. Hovering a slice or a legend row highlights the other.

Detail view

  • Detail - Aging, Detail - Customers: the Aging / Customers switch is top-right. The chosen view shows as a table on the left ("By aging" / "By customer") and a "Share of total" pie on the right. V1 always showed "By aging" bars and "Top overdue customers" bars side by side, with no switch.

Pop-up

  • How it opens: click a band or customer row in a table, or its entry in a pie legend. V1 opened from the bars.
  • Pop-up - Invoices, header: a subtitle under the title ("band · N customers · $total") replaces V1's band chip. Export to Excel and the close × are unchanged.
  • Pop-up - Invoices, search: new "Search customer or invoice #" box. It matches either.
  • Pop-up - Invoices, columns: Customer, Bill To, Due Date, Invoice #, Days Past Due, Outstanding, every heading sortable. Default is Days Past Due, oldest first. There is no separate sort control.
  • Pop-up - Invoices, list: the list uses the full width, one row per invoice, with a footer row "N invoices … $total". V1 had the summary on the right. There are no row checkboxes and no select-all.
  • Pop-up - Invoices, buttons: Close only. V1's "Confirm" is gone; nothing replaces it.
  • Pop-up - Invoice expanded: a contact line above the tabs: the customer's email and phone.
  • Pop-up - Invoice expanded: tabs are Details, Attachments and Note. The Payments tab is gone.
  • Pop-up - Invoice expanded: one button, Open invoice. V1's "Record a follow-up" is gone.

Developer Notes

Nothing here needs a new backend read. Every figure is derived from the invoices V1 already returns (customer, bill-to, invoice number, due date, outstanding amount) plus the as-of date. The only front-end addition is the pie, and it plots the same band and customer totals as the table beside it.

Aging bands

  • Days past due = the as-of date minus the invoice's due date.
  • Bands: Current = 0 or less (not yet due); 1-30; 31-60; 61-90; 91-120; 121+. V1's Current covered everything up to 30 days past due, so the same invoice can sit in a different band than it did in V1.
  • Show all six bands, including empty ones.

Overdue badge

  • Overdue = the total of every invoice with days past due above 0. % = overdue ÷ total outstanding, rounded to a whole number. Example: $78,390 ÷ $84,570 = 92.7%, shown as 93%.
  • It follows the revenue center and source filters, the same as the total.

Customers view

  • Balance = the sum of the customer's open invoices under the current filters. Aging = days past due of the customer's oldest open invoice, "Current" when none is past due. The dot colour is that invoice's band.

Pie

  • Aging pie: one slice per band with a balance, in band order. Customers pie: one slice per customer, largest first. % = slice ÷ total outstanding. A band or customer with $0 is left out of the pie but stays in the table.
  • Slice and legend row highlight each other on hover; clicking either opens the same pop-up as the table row.

Pop-up

  • Search matches the customer name or the invoice number, and applies before the sort.
  • Sorting is on the column headers: money and days columns open highest first, text columns A to Z; a second click reverses. Default is Days Past Due, highest first, ties by amount.
  • The contact line needs the customer's email and phone, which the customer record already holds. No last-contact history is shown.
  • Export to Excel downloads the rows shown, under the pop-up's search and sort.

Data

  • Two reads per purpose, nothing else.   • Posted and not-undone are part of every figure, not a filter: on the owner's dev database the excluded lines were $30,192 against $200 included.
  • Per gift line: date, amount, pledge link, how it arrived, reference (cheque or receipt number).
  • Today is the browser's date; the demos pin it (V1 24 Jul 2026, V2 19 Aug 2026).

The counting rule

  • A pledge's position is always read to the thru date (the window's end), whatever the start; gift transactions obey the whole window. So the Pledges table and the headline percentage never move with a start date; Giving, the Gifts table and the ledger do.
  • Presets. Gifts received through: no start, end = chosen date (today by default). Year to date: 1 January to today (never "fiscal"; gifts run on the calendar). This quarter, This month: first day to today. The default has no start so the Pledges table matches the legacy panel figure for figure and old pledges catch up: 2020 Pledge, $1,000 pledged, term long ended, $96 received → Pledge Due $1,000, Due Remaining $904, Percent Due 90.40%.
  • Pledge Due per pledge: the full pledge once the thru date is past its end date; otherwise the instalment amount × (cycles elapsed from the begin date to the thru date + 1), capped at the instalment count. Example: $12,000 monthly over 24 months from 1 Jan 2025, thru 19 Aug 2026 → $500 × (19 + 1) = $10,000. Count cycles to the chosen date, not today; the legacy routine anchors on today, a known defect.
  • Due Remaining = Pledge Due − pledge payments to the thru date; negative is over-received, in parentheses (Stoke Sell: $1,200 pledged, $1,855 received → ($655.00)). Percent Due = Due Remaining ÷ Pledge Due; "n/a" when Pledge Due is zero (the legacy divides by 1 there). Not totalled.
  • Headline pill = pledge payments ÷ Pledge Total ($325,951 ÷ $400,200 = 81.45%), only with no start date. Status moves on pledge payments alone: Fully funded when payments ≥ Pledge Total, On track when ≥ Pledge Due, else Behind; gifts with no pledge is "Gifts only, no pledge".
  • Bar: pledge payments in the window; still expected = Pledge Total − pledge payments, never below zero; gifts in the window. Scale = the larger of Pledge Total and payments, plus gifts, so an over-paid pledge cannot run off the track.
  • Tables and ledger: Pledges lists purposes with an active pledge, largest Pledge Total first (the panel's order). Gifts lists purposes with an unlinked gift in the window; Donors counts a donor once per purpose, once across purposes in the totals row. The ledger is the same two reads for one purpose, sorted by amount. Six figures are needed from the general ledger: income, expenses and net income for the current fiscal year to date, and the same three for the prior fiscal year to date. Everything else is presentation.

Data

  • The three values and their prior-year values. The demo's figures ($1,824,350 against $1,682,400; $1,681,550 against $1,598,200; $142,800 against $84,200) are fixtures copied verbatim from the spec, and so are its percentages.

  2. Groups. One collapsible group per alert category, colour-edged by severity, opening to its items.
