## 3. Our diff doc

### 3a. Jo vs Oisin - Design Differences

Ported 2026-09-08 against Jo's `phase-2` @ `71ca056` on branch `oisin-v2-rebuild`. **First-ever port of this widget.** Ours is `kind:"payables-mb"`, prefix `apF`/`APF_`, entry `apFContent`, CSS root `.apf-*`, actions `data-apf` with `apF-` values, six registry entries on the `(OC)` convention. Source of truth: our built Final `apF`, `FC_VERSION[16]` = 2.0, built 2026-08-19 as a 1-to-1 copy of Jo's own v2 `ap` design (her dossier 7371554882 Part C decisions 11.1 / 11.3 / 11.4 / 11.5) plus **one** owner-approved addition, and re-verified against the build by `widget-final-check-audit` on 2026-09-07. Because ours starts as her design, the delta below is small and almost every row is either the horizon addition or a consequence of it.

**Collision note, the sharpest on this project so far.** Jo's own payables widget is `kind:"payables"` with **38** `ap*`-prefixed helpers, and **four of them begin with the same three letters as our prefix**: `apFmtLong`, `apFmtShort`, `apFull` and, alongside them, `apSelLabel`. This is the same trap as `ptoFindEmp` on W09 and `purFiltered` on W13, and it is worse here because the letters match exactly. The rule that clears it is that every one of our names carries a **doubled F**: ours are `apFFmtLong`, `apFFmtShort`, `apFFull` and `apFSelLabel`, so no name of ours can shadow one of hers. Before this port `APF_` and `.apf-` each had **zero** occurrences in her file. Verified after it: all 38 of her helpers are still defined exactly once, every one of their definition lines is byte-identical to the pre-edit snapshot, the raw occurrence counts of all four lookalikes are unchanged (6 / 2 / 2 / 4), her `AP_INVOICES`, `AP_INVOICES_SINGLE`, `AP_BANDS` and `AP_TODAY` are each declared exactly once, her six `kind:"payables"` registry rows are untouched, and her `EMPTY_COPY.payables` and `ERROR_COPY.payables` lines are unedited (our empty-state strings are inline in our own block rather than written into her shared copy object). Not one of the 57 top-level names our block defines equals one of hers. Her data-actions stay `ap-*` and ours are `data-apf="apF-*"`, so her delegated click listener never sees ours and ours never sees hers.

**This port REUSES her CSS almost entirely.** She already declares the whole `.ap-*` cluster our markup draws with, the two-line due cell and the tinted band subheader included, plus every shell primitive the widget needs. Our CSS block therefore adds only **nine** rules, all `.apf-` scoped, and declares **zero** of her selectors. The single rule that mentions one of hers, `.apf-root .ap-sum-row[data-apf]:hover`, is scoped under our root first and exists only because her own hover rule keys on `[data-action]`, which our selector rows deliberately do not carry.

Verified by `w16-payables-mb.driver.js`, 740/740. All twelve previously ported drivers re-run green afterwards.

**Open items carried, not resolved.**
- **THE AGING BASIS WAS NEVER CONFIRMED AGAINST THE REAL API, and it is load-bearing for every one of the four bands.** This widget bands on the invoice **due date**, which is what her `apBandOf` does and what the Step 1 research describes, but whether the real aging should run from the due date or the **invoice date** has never been checked against the API. If it turns out to be the invoice date, every band boundary, every band subtotal, the overdue count, the overdue pill, the cash-requirements panel and the horizon itself all move. It is this widget's single largest open question and the reason the band maths is confined to one function. The related unresolved detail is Step 4 Sign-off row 2: a mislabeling bug confirmed on W10 ("Over 60" actually meaning 90 or more days) may apply here too and was never confirmed per widget, so any future banded label needs checking before reuse.
- **Actionability is DISPUTED and both sides stay recorded.** Jo and the SME (dossier 11.2) want pay and schedule actions on the widget. **Feargal ruled on 2026-08-25** that it stays a focused due-date and aging view and must not become "a container for every payment function", preferring separate action widgets for unposted invoices, payment processing and posting to the general ledger, which are assigned to Aditya and outside this project's queue. **This port is READ-ONLY, per that ruling:** no pay, schedule, approve or write control exists anywhere in the block, and the driver asserts their absence. Neither side is treated as the correction of the other.
- **Dossier 11.3 / 11.4 and 11.6 are Unreviewed, and no reconciliation file exists for this widget.** 11.3 and 11.4 concern a date-ordered chart with the table synced to it; **the Final has no chart at all**, and what the build does instead is realise the sync as synced values-as-text, the cash-requirements band rows doubling as the due filter, rather than as an unsynced chart. 11.6, pairing this widget with Bank Balances on the dashboard, is a layout question that nothing here can settle. None of Jo's flags on this widget carries an Accepted, Rejected or Disputed status yet.
- **Entitlement is not enforced by the backend.** The Modern API treats this widget's module access as metadata only: any authenticated user can call the endpoint whether or not their organisation holds an Accounts Payable licence. Step 4 records the no-rights state as "not yet specified", so **nothing is simulated here**; it is NEW backend work and must be fixed server-side before release (Sign-off row 3).
- **The localisation defect and data freshness are NEW and UNVERIFIED backend work.** The legacy widget hard-codes a pound sign for a US organisation. Our block formats en-US dollars, which is right for this shell's demo org, but Step 4 records "Rounding / currency / locale rules: *not yet specified*", so the format is a build choice rather than a decision. There is no "data as of" signal in either version and no freshness rule anywhere; refresh is only specified as preserving the current selection.
- **Table semantics are ported AS BUILT and flagged, not silently upgraded.** The hero table is **div-based** (`.wt-row`), with no `table` or `columnheader` roles and no `th`/`scope`, exactly as she built it. The filter chips and sort buttons do carry `aria-label`s and focus-visible styles, and both popovers use `role="listbox"` with `role="option"`. Whether to require `role=table` parity, as **W11** built and as **W15** went further and used native `<table>` elements for, is undecided and is marked `[TO CONFIRM - Oisin]` in Step 4. It is recorded here precisely because it is the one place this port knowingly ships less accessibility than two of its siblings.
- **Drill-through stays an open question, with nothing built.** A link out to the full Accounts Payable module, filtered to the same due date or vendor, is "leaning yes" but has no verified target page or URL pattern and has never been checked `[LIVE]`. The Final was built without any drill-through element and so is this port (Sign-off row 1).
- **The by-vendor donut was considered and deferred, not rejected.** The competitor research (NetSuite, Coefficient) surfaced reorienting the by-date donut to a by-vendor cut. It was not adopted in this lock because it would add a grouping dimension no original concept modelled, and because the Final has no chart to reorient. The Detail panel's Top vendors owed rollup is the by-vendor cut the build does carry. Owner is TBD if the idea is revived.
- **The urgency-bucket boundary maths was the one open item that got resolved,** on 2026-08-19 by owner instruction adopting her band maths wholesale. It is noted here only so a future reader does not reopen it: the "Overdue Now / Due This Week / Due This Month" ambiguity in the older documents (calendar week versus rolling 7 days, and whether the month bucket includes the week one) is settled by her `apBandOf` and its four non-overlapping bands.
- **Behaviour of an empty urgency bucket was unspecified and is a recorded build choice.** An emptied band renders as a **disabled row saying "nothing due"** in the cash panel, and is **omitted** from both the due-date popover and the table's band subheaders. Hidden-versus-zero was never decided, so the panel keeps the row visible for a stable layout while the list and the table drop it to avoid offering a selection that would empty the table.
- **The related `ap-ar-aging` Phase 2 widget uses different buckets.** The Modern API defines `GET /api/dashboard/ap-ar-aging`, combining this widget's AP side with W05's AR side on Current / 1-30 / 31-60 / 61-90 / 91+ boundaries, which are **not** this widget's four due-date bands. Informational only, and not part of this widget's contract, but worth reconciling if the two are ever unified.
