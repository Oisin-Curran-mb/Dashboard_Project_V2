**Glance.** Ours: the all-accounts total only, label "Total balance, all N bank accounts", an Overdrawn pill if the total is negative; no controls, no sparkline, no delta. Jo's: account chip on top (opens the real picker), the amount in whole dollars, a "N overdrawn ›" pill that drills into Detail filtered to overdrawn accounts, and a running-balance sparkline (Beginning to Ending across the reconciliation entries, green/red dots, hover card with Amount, Balance, Since beginning). With an account selected her Glance shows that account. Her PR feedback item G12 (below-the-fold clipping) is about this sparkline.

**Explore, all accounts.** Headline: ours short money ("$10.8M", exact in title) with a context sentence "52 active accounts, ordered alphabetically. Balances are the running tally…"; Jo's the full amount plus a muted "Unreconciled" chip. Chips: both have account and "N overdrawn"; ours reads "N overdrawn only" and resets paging, hers also clears the selected account. Table: ours a real `<table>`, Account / Balance, A to Z fixed, server paging 12 per page ("1 to 12 of 52", "Page 1 of 5"); Jo's div rows with a search box, Account (name over masked number) / Balance, sortable (`bank-sort`, default balance desc), Load more past 20 ("Showing X of Y"). Bars: same diverging component; ours amethyst, zero axis, legend, header row, paged A to Z; hers red negatives, sorted by the table key, unpaged, names clickable. Clicks: ours none; hers `bank-acct-drill` opens a Detail clone in a modal with a back button.

**Explore, one account.** Ours: ending balance, a sentence on where the beginning balance came from, no toggle, seven-row table Line / Amount in fixed order with explanation lines, Ending Balance as row 7. Jo's: ending balance, Overdrawn pill, Unreconciled chip, Table/Bars toggle kept, `bankSingleTable` Type / Amount sortable, Beginning pinned on top, signed amounts, Ending balance in a total strip.

**Detail.** Ours: Explore with more room; single account shows "Seven row breakdown" beside "Activity categories" (Deposits, Voids, Checks, Withdrawals, In/Out labels, no hover). Jo's: no toggle, both views side by side (`bankAllFull` table with a Type column + comparison bars; `bankSingleFull` movement table + "Activity by type" bars with red out-bars and a hover card).

**States.** Ours: empty ("No active bank accounts"), missing account; no loading or error. Jo's: empty with "Add bank account", error with Retry, 850 ms loading skeleton on account change.

**Only ours:** server paging; totals and bar scale over the whole filtered set; overdrawn count over the whole set; header/footer wording follows the overdrawn filter; spoken mode announcement; explanation lines; bar column header; never-reconciled flag; 52 accounts; seven registry variants.

**Only Jo's:** sparkline; Unreconciled chip; column sort, search, Load more; picker search that matches type; masked numbers and Type column; drill modal and Glance drill; loading and error states; CSV/Excel/PDF export actions; both views side by side at Detail.

**Computed differently.** Ending balance: both sum beginning plus five activity types. Her sparkline labels the EFT step "Ending balance", so EFT never shows as a movement. Money: ours 2 decimals en-US, abbreviated in tables; hers whole dollars via `toLocaleString()` with no locale (browser-dependent separators). Neither shows available cash, in-transit, or a date range.

**Jo's PR feedback vs ours.** Accepted and present: real table, red off the bars, totals over the whole set, 52 accounts. Rejected and still in ours: server paging, no sort, subtitle without delta.
