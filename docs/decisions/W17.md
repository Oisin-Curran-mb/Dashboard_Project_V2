### What the code settled

The legacy system holds two models and the owner's two instincts each described one. The dashboard panel is pledge-only: `GFPledgeRepository.GetWidgetData` walks `GFPledge.GFHistoryDetails` on `PledgeID`. The parallel purpose-wide path, `GFPurpose.GFHistoryDetails` on `PurposeID`, exists and is simply unused by that panel. A real goal does exist, but it is `GFCampaignDetail.Goal` per dated period, and the Campaign screen measures it against every `CampaignID`-tagged gift.

Our build had blended the two: a campaign-style goal measured against pledge-linked receipts only. The data does not support that. The owner's ruling to count both resolves it.

### Decided

- **The row unit is the purpose**, not the campaign. A campaign is a real and different entity, many per purpose, each with dated goal periods.
- **Received carries both parts**, named **Pledge payments** and **Other gifts** in the bar, the legend, the ledger and the pop-up summary. The column keeps the name Received.
- **The invented per-purpose goal is retired**, with its 75 and 100 per cent bands, labels and colours. No such field exists per purpose.
- **Pledge Total, Pledge Due, Due Remaining and Percent Due are unchanged.** All four already matched the legacy panel's formulas, and keeping them is what lets the table reconcile.
- **Every money figure obeys the date window, and the default preset has no start date.** This was the one call made on the owner's behalf, and it is the reason the default still reconciles with the shipped panel figure for figure on the pledge-payments subtotal. A start date with an unwindowed Pledge Due would make a fully paid old pledge look catastrophically behind.
- Presets: Gifts received through (default), Year to date, This quarter, This month, Custom dates. The word "fiscal" is avoided, on Edward Eoff's instruction for the sibling widget: it implies the General Ledger year, and gifts run on the calendar.
- **"Paid in full" on a pledge that is merely current becomes "Up to date."** The old label was wrong.

### Defects fixed

- **Per-pledge pace was all-or-nothing.** Received counted a pledge's whole paid amount only if its date fell inside the window. It now sums gift lines.
- **The pop-up did not redraw on expand.** `gopen` called `render()` only, and the modal is body-mounted, so opening a row did nothing. Present since the 25 September port. This is the defect the owner remembered.
- **The Detail drill was window-blind.** It paced every donor pledge over all time while the purpose row above it obeyed the window, so at a narrow preset the two levels of one widget disagreed. Both now read the same window.
- Reference is fed from the real fields, `GFHistory.CheckNumber` and `ReceiptNumber`. This corrects Part 10 of the review document, which wrongly said the column had no source.
- Dead code removed: the retired donut, the goal panel, the pre-v1.2 layout branch. The two undeclared classes declared. Nine ledger rules root-scoped. `gpf-seg-other-tag` declared from the same token as the bar's other-gifts segment.

### New in the data

Unpledged gifts per purpose, gift media, motivation with code and name, and references. All real fields on the gift row. A seventh purpose, Memorial Gifts, has gifts and no pledge at all, which the old widget could not show. Open: whether the dashboard endpoint can supply media and motivation. The fields exist; the API spec does not request them yet.

### The pop-up

The "donors behind pace" modal is replaced by a giving ledger: everything that came in for the purpose, largest first, with a type filter (All, Pledge payments, Other gifts), a search box matching donor, pledge and reference, and each row expanding to its history. A pledge shows its gifts with date, how it arrived, what prompted it, reference and amount. The old modal duplicated W04 Remittance Pledges.

### Still open

- The real campaign goal, which needs a campaign-period row unit.
- Our W04 pacing basis differs from the legacy monthly step. Recorded in Part 10 of the review document.
- One stray edit: a `campaign` to `purpose` rename in the rebuild reached a comment inside Jo's own block ("capital campaign" became "capital purpose"). Her code is unaffected and her block is deleted at finalisation.

## 28 Sep: "Prompted by" dropped, and what the legacy code says about it

Owner: "drop Prompted by until we know it is wanted or needed because I dont think this is needed."

### What the legacy code actually holds

Checked in `MBAccounting`, read-only:

- `GFHistory.MotivationID` is real, a nullable foreign key, captured by a picker on Unposted Gifts, Update Gift. The panel is hidden entirely when no motivations are set up.
- `GFMotivation` is a proper appeal record: code, name, start and end dates, cost, a correspondence-sent flag, and `PurposeID`.
- **It is not on the gift line.** `GFHistoryDetail`, which carries `PledgeID` and `PurposeID`, has no motivation column. Same for media: `MediaID` is on `GFHistory`, and appears zero times on the detail.
- The legacy Gift List report does show it against a line, by reaching up to the gift: `x.GFHistory.GFMotivation.Code`. So our display was faithful, not invented.
- Motivation also sits on `GFPledge`, and that is the one place the application makes a label of it, `PurposeMotivation` as purpose slash motivation, plus a pledge filter. We never showed that.

### Why dropping it is right

A motivation belongs to at most one purpose, while the picker offers every motivation in the company and the value sits on the gift header. A gift split across purposes therefore carries one motivation for all its lines, and it may belong to a purpose other than the line being read. This pop-up is scoped to one purpose, so it could print a motivation belonging elsewhere and never admit it.

Our fixture also overstated it: every gift always carried one of four motivations, where the real field is nullable and blank for any church that does not run appeals.

### What was done

The column is off both gift tables, which drop from five columns to four, and off the gift row's aria-label. It also leaves the ledger's search haystack, so the search matches only what is on screen; media stays in it, because "Arrived by" is still shown.

"Until" is the operative word, so the model keeps the field. `GPF_MOTIVATIONS` and the `motive` and `motiveCode` on each gift are still seeded, and the driver asserts they are, so bringing the column back is two spans and a grid template rather than a data change.

### What was wrong with the rebuild earlier the same day

The rebuild read the 19 August ruling as "blend them", and blended them everywhere:
`gpFPurposeCompute` returned one `received` of pledge payments plus unpledged gifts, and the
Glance pill, the bars, the percentage and the Summary Table's Received column all read it.
Two figures could therefore not be traced to a single purpose-keyed query.

- **Received** mixed money that answers to a pledge with money that does not, so it no longer
  equalled the shipped panel's Received column, and a purpose could pass 100 per cent of its
  pledges on the strength of a gift that was never pledged.
- **Pledge Total** was re-scoped by `gpFPledgedIn` to "the instalments falling inside the
  window" on any preset with a start date. That proration exists nowhere in the data.

Both were mine, not the owner's. They are reversed.

### Confirmed against the real database, not inferred

The owner ran three queries on `A90911DB` (dev, `ssdevdevsql01`) while this was being built:

| Question | Result |
|---|---|
| Do unpledged gifts to a purpose exist? | Yes. Purpose `2022 GIF`, $200.00 across two lines, $0.00 of pledge payments. |
| Does a purpose exist that received gifts and has no active pledge? | Yes, the same purpose, and its `AllowPledges` is **1**: it permits pledges and simply has none active. |
| Is there money that must not count? | Yes. Five lines, **$30,192.00**, in unposted batches. No undone gifts in that database. |

The third answer is the one that matters most: in that database the excluded money is 150 times
the included money. The posted and not-undone tests are part of the definition of every figure
here, not a detail of the query.

The fixture was corrected to match the second answer. `MEMGIFT` now carries
`allowPledges:true` with no active pledge, which is the shape the real row has; the
`AllowPledges` column is still honoured in `gpFDonorsFor` and is asserted directly rather than
through a fixture row.

### The rule the widget now obeys

Every figure comes from one of two queries, both keyed on `PurposeID`, and nothing else is shown.

**Q1, the purpose's gift lines.** `GFHistoryDetail` where `PurposeID` is the row's purpose,
`GFHistory.GFBatch.Posted` is true, `GFHistory.UnDoJournalID` is null and `GiftDate` falls in
the window. Split once, on `PledgeID`: not null is a **pledge payment**, null is a **gift**.

**Q2, the purpose's pledges.** `GFPledge` where `PurposeID` is the row's purpose and `Active`
is true. Pledge Total is `Sum(Pledge)`; Pledge Due is `Sum(PledgeDue(true, thru))`.

No campaign anything: `CampaignID` and `GFCampaignDetail.Goal` stay out, and the Campaign
screen's figures are never copied here, because that screen omits the posted and not-undone
filters.

A pledge's position is always read to the thru date, because a pledge is a cumulative promise.
Gift transactions obey the whole window. That distinction is what the row shape carries:

| Figure | Means |
|---|---|
| `fromPledgesThru` | pledge payments to the thru date: the legacy panel's Received |
| `fromPledges` | the same money narrowed to the window: what Giving shows |
| `other` | gifts with no pledge, inside the window |
| `totalIn` | both: all the money given to the purpose |
| `pledgeTotal` | the full active pledge, never re-scoped |
| `pledgeDue` | the legacy proration on the thru date |
| `dueRem`, `percentDue` | the legacy formulas, off `fromPledgesThru` |
| `fulfilled` | `fromPledgesThru` over `pledgeTotal`, a pledge-lifetime read |
| `windowed` | true when the preset has a start date |

There is no `received` property any more, so nothing can read a blended figure by accident.
`gpFPledgedIn` is deleted: its only job was the re-scoping now reversed, and `gpFScheduleRows`
already steps a pledge identically for the schedule panel.

### The table, two reads, one at a time

A second toggle sits in the header's top right beside the view toggle, and only while the table
view is up. Her `.vtoggle` chrome, text only, because two toggles share that row at Explore and
an icon apiece does not fit. Pledges is the default.

**Pledges** is `GFPledgeRepository.GetWidgetData`. Rows are purposes with at least one active
pledge and nothing else, so a purpose that takes gifts and pledges nothing is absent, exactly
as it is absent from the panel, whose query root is `GFPledge`. Ordered by Pledge Total
descending, which is the panel's own final `OrderByDescending` and not a user control, so the
widget still offers no ordering of its own. Received is pledge-linked money only: no gift money
reaches a cell, a sub-line or the totals row. **A preset's start date does not act here**, which
is what keeps the mode one to one; the chip states the date that does.

**Gifts** is the money the panel cannot see: gift lines with a null `PledgeID`, on the Purpose
List report's own vocabulary (`PurposeListViewModel` counts gifts, givers and totals per
purpose). Columns are Purpose, Gifts, Donors, Last gift, Total. The whole window acts here,
start and end, because these are transactions. Donors are counted once each across purposes. A
row opens the giving ledger already filtered to gifts.

Each mode totals its own rows through `gpFSumRows`, so a totals line can never include a
purpose its table does not show.

### Giving is unchanged, with one correction

The bars, the two segments, the hovers, the legend and the ordering stay as built: Giving is
"all the money given to a purpose", which is `totalIn`, and it is strictly purpose-scoped. Two
figures changed because they were not defensible:

- The percentage is pledge payments over the amount pledged, so an unpledged gift can no longer
  push a purpose past 100 per cent of its pledges. On Building Fund it reads 82.00 per cent
  ($205,000 of $250,000) where the blended figure read 94.60 per cent.
- **On a windowed preset no percentage of pledged is shown at all.** With a start date the money
  on screen is this window's while the pledge behind it is the whole promise, and a ratio across
  the two dates is a number the data does not hold. The pledged amount itself still shows. This
  replaces the 28 September "one call made on the owner's behalf", which solved the same problem
  by re-scoping the pledge instead.

### Three defects the browser found, which reading the markup would not have

- **A bare percent that no longer matched the line under it.** A Giving row printed "82.00%"
  over the foot line "$236,500 received of $250,000 pledged". While Received was blended the two
  agreed; now they cannot, because the percent measures the dark segment alone. It reads
  "82.00% paid", hovers with both amounts, and says so to a screen reader.
- **The Pledges row's sub-line did not fit, and said the wrong kind of thing.** Measured: the
  name cell is 81px at Explore, its sub-line had about 57px of usable width, and the shortest
  honest wording needed 68px. It also put a second, differently-based percentage beside Percent
  Due: two numbers about one pledge, each correct, inviting the wrong one. The panel's own row
  has no sub-line, and this mode exists to match it, so it is gone. The Gifts row keeps its
  own, which measures 114px in a 114px slot and says the one thing its columns do not.
- **The purpose name had 57px of the 166px it needs.** Six columns at 82px plus a 70px percent
  column in a 568px card left nothing for the name, and every row read "BLDGF...". Narrowing
  the money columns is the wrong trade: a clipped money figure on a tax-tracked table is a worse
  defect than a clipped name, and real data holds larger amounts than this fixture. **Percent
  Due is now a Detail column.** It is the one column derivable from two others in the same row,
  it stays in every row's screen-reader line at every size, and the name went from 57px to
  136px. Both captions also lost a sentence; the Pledges one was repeating the date that sits in
  the chip beside it.

### Deliberate divergences from the panel, recorded

- **Percent Due when Pledge Due is zero.** The legacy POCO divides by 1 in that case, which
  prints the negative of Received as a percentage. This shows "n/a" instead. No fixture row hits
  it; on real data a purpose whose pledges all begin in the future would.
- **Pledge Due anchors on the selected date**, not on `DateTime.Today`. The legacy routine counts
  cycles from today in every branch and only tests the end date against the chosen one, which
  Step 3 called a known defect and the API spec tells the backend not to replicate. Unchanged
  from before today.
- **A purpose with gifts and no active pledge gets a row** in Gifts and a bar in Giving. The
  panel cannot show it at all. This is the point of the change.
