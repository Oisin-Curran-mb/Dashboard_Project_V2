## Diff, ours vs Jo's phase-2 (8958e49), 28 Sep 2026

**Glance.** Ours: fixed "Gifts and Pledges" chip (ignores the campaign filter), Received in whole dollars, a band pill ("Goal Met" or NN.NN%) then "of $X goal", a small progress bar, caption "<band> across N campaigns", no clicks. Jo's: chip shows the current purpose, Received, a status pill with a dollar figure ("$X behind", "$X ahead", "Fully funded", "On track") then "NN% of goal", a bar with a due-by-now marker and a popover, caption "N purposes behind schedule" or "On track or ahead across every purpose".

**Explore.** Ours: Campaign chip (All campaigns + "CODE: Name"), Date range chip (This year, Last 30 days, Custom From/To), Goal Progress / Summary Table toggle, Export stub. Goal Progress: bars sorted closest-to-goal first with percent, "$ received of $ goal", band badge, a legend with counts per band and "Remaining to goal overall"; bar click opens the modal. Summary Table: Purpose, Pledge Total, Pledge Due, Received, Due Remaining, Percent Due; no sorting; Closed badge; row click expands an inline donor drill (20 per page, pager, Export stub) with Name, Begin, End, Pledge, Received, Due Remaining, Status; pledge row expands its gifts (Gift Date, Amount, Reference, total). Jo's: Date chip (Today, End of last month, one custom date), Purpose chip hidden with one purpose, Progress / Table toggle. Progress rows use her bar anatomy (received fill, hatched expected shortfall, due marker, % fulfilled, flag), legend "On track / Behind / Expected by today". Table: Purpose, Pledged, Due by date, Received, Remaining, % Fulfilled, every header sortable (default fulfilled ascending), totals row with %. Bars and rows open the modal.

**Detail.** Ours: same as Explore with the bar list uncapped. Jo's: no toggle; two columns, "Progress toward goal" and a "Behind schedule" follow-up list (purpose, % fulfilled, donor count, "$ behind") whose rows open the modal filtered to Behind; "Nothing to follow up" when empty.

**The modal.** Ours: title "CODE: Name", sub "donor pledges most behind pace"; KPI strip Pledge Total, Pledge Due, Received, Due Remaining, Goal ($ and %) with band badge; top five behind (Due Remaining > 0) with a note; columns Name, Begin, End, Pledge, Received, Due Remaining, Status; row expands to the gift list with Reference; footer "Open in Gifts and Pledges" stub and Close; opened from goal bars only. Jo's: title "Gifts Pledges: Purpose", sub "as of date"; no KPI strip; All donors / Behind only toggle; columns Donor, Pledged, Received, Due by date, Remaining, Last gift, Status; row opens a drawer (Pledged / Received / Still to give, gift history, "Open donor record"); footer with donor count and received/behind totals, Export to Excel in the header; opened from bars, table rows and follow-up rows.

**Only ours:** a separate campaign goal with three bands (In progress, Close to goal at 75%+, Goal Met); a date range; paging; pledge-to-gift inline drill; Reference column; Closed badge; Export stubs; a preselected one-campaign fixture.
**Only Jo's:** sortable headers; Behind-schedule panel and due-by-now marker; donor drawer with "Still to give" and "Open donor record"; the all-on-track fixture; empty and error copy entries.

**Computed differently.** Pledge total: ours sums per-pledge amounts, hers sums the schedule and calls it the goal. Received: ours counts a pledge's paid amount only if its gift date is on or before the range end; hers sums dated gifts to the thru-date. Due: ours is an instalment-cycle formula ("Pledge Due"); hers is scheduled instalments up to the date ("Due by date"). Pace: ours bands by days ahead or behind (30, 60); hers is the sign of due minus received. Percent: ours shows Percent Due and goal progress; hers % Fulfilled. Anchor date: ours 19 Aug 2026, hers 24 Jul 2026.

**Defects in hers.** Undeclared `gft-full`, `gft-empty`, `gft-barrow-static`; dead handler `gft-followup` and several dead rules; duplicate or conflicting rules for `.gft-panel`, `.gft-panel-h`, `.gft-glance-cap`, `.gft-glance-read`, `.modal.gft-detail-modal`; her table totals row lacks `wt-row`, so its padding may not line up.
