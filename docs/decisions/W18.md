## 2026-09-28: Aditya's latest, and the Cash Position tile dropped

Owner: "I need Aditya KPI version to update mine to his latest", then "drop the
cash postion completly you can port it over and then remove or just not include
the code. what ever is better", and take all four of his layout changes.

### Which version is "his latest"

Two commits on his `feature/aditya-widget-design` branch, both 2026-09-25, in
`Aditya_Widget_Design/Demo V2.html`:

| Commit | What it did |
|---|---|
| `c0359df` | remove Cash Position tile from Financial KPI Variant A heading band |
| `c016583` | Variant A: equidistant 3-tile layout, remove prototype cycle button |

So his latest is a **subtraction** from what we had, not an addition. This was
worth establishing rather than assuming: the four-tile band with the warning
chip was ours, and the three-card band was his, which is the opposite of the
reading the request first suggests. Our `_ref/aditya/Demo V2.html` is
byte-identical to his branch file (md5 `ef826306`), so the reference in the
repo is already his latest and needed no refresh.

Nothing else of his is newer than ours. His Variant A still lacks the body
mounted panel, the design-system tokens and the shared `escText` that our port
added, and his file also carries Variants B, C and D plus a
`window.fkpVariantSwitch` to flip between them. Those are exploration
scaffolding for his own review and stay out; the driver asserts none of them
reached our build.

### The four changes taken

1. Three tiles, not four. Cash Position goes.
2. `.fkp-row`: `repeat(3,1fr)` with a 10px gap, and no padding or background of
   its own.
3. `.fkp-tile`: its own surface, border and 8px radius, replacing the 1px
   divider band. The dividers made sense when a fourth tile carried a warning
   that had to sit visually inside the band; with three equal cards they do not.
4. `.fkp-band`: `height:auto`, replacing Jo's fixed 176px Glance height, with
   `.fkp-root` padding the band and `.fkp-hd` only padding beneath itself.

Measured at 1440px after the change: three cards of 369px at identical tops
with exactly 10px between each pair, band 147px tall, no overflow.

### The reconciliation warning was deleted, not left dormant

The owner asked for the tile to go completely and left the method open. It is
**deleted**, because this repo lints for declared-and-unused CSS and for product
code kept alive only by a test, and because the subsystem could never have been
reached again: `.fkp-warn` lived inside `.fkp-cash`, and the prototype chip was
the only way to move it off its default state. Keeping it would have been
keeping something no user could ever see.

Removed from the JavaScript: `FKP_CASH`, `FKP_THRESHOLD_DAYS`, `FKP_ACCTS`,
`FKP_DEMO`, `fkpSevOf`, `fkpUndefinedBand`, `fkpAcctHTML`, `fkpPopContent`,
`fkpWarnHTML`, `fkpCashHTML`, `fkpClosePop`, `fkpOpenPop`, `fkpInSurface`,
`fkpPopShow`, `fkpPopHide`, `fkpPopHideSoon`, `fkpCycle`, `FKP_HIDE_MS`,
`_fkpHideT`, and the `demo`, `accts` and `popOpen` keys of `FKP_STATE`.

Removed from the CSS: `.fkp-demo*`, `.fkp-unit`, `.fkp-info*`, `.fkp-warn*`,
`.fkp-cash[data-fkp-sev]`, `.fkp-pop*`, `.fkp-tip-hd`, `.fkp-tip-body`,
`.fkp-tip-cta`, `.fkp-acct*`, and the `--sf-20/60/100/140`, `--red-50` and
`--pos-30` tokens the chip and the panel were the only users of. `--cn-90`
stays: the tile label and the middle-dot separator still use it.

Removed from the wiring: the `mouseover`, `mouseout`, `keydown`, `resize` and
`scroll` listeners, the outside-click branch, and the `warn`, `acct`, `review`,
`cycle` and `stop` actions. **One click listener with one action survives**, for
refresh, where rev 2 had five listeners and six actions.

Confirmed still working after the removal: refresh shows nine skeleton bars,
three per card, hides the figures, keeps itself available, then restores the
figures, resets the stamp to "just now" and reports "Financial KPI refreshed."
through Jo's `setStatus`. One column at 600px with no horizontal overflow.

### What this loses, recorded because the code no longer says it

Aditya's v5 spec sections 5, 6, 7, 8, 10 and 11 no longer apply to this block.
The substance worth keeping:

- **The reconciliation warning existed to qualify the cash figure.** Its claim
  was that a cash position is only as good as the last reconciliation. With the
  tile gone, the band makes no cash claim, so there is nothing left to qualify.
  If Cash Position ever returns, the warning is the thing to bring back with it.
- **Spec 5a left the 3-to-5-account band undefined** and flagged it as needing
  product confirmation. The prototype treated it as amber and said so on screen.
  That question is now moot here, but it is a real product question and this is
  the only place it was written down.
- **The threshold was 30 days, hardcoded**, and spec section 11 recorded it as
  an admin setting in production.
- **The account rows and the red-state call to action both navigated nowhere**,
  by design: there was no verified target in Bank Account Management, so they
  stated the intent through `setStatus` instead of claiming a route.

Restoring the tile is not a two-line change, unlike W17's dropped column: the
model, the panel, the hover manager and the wiring would all have to come back.
`_tools/one-off/build-w18-aditya-latest-2026-09-28.js` holds the exact text of
everything removed, and the commit before it is the last build that had it.

### One deliberate divergence from his file

`.fkp-tile` keeps `position:relative` in his Variant A. We drop it: it existed
only to anchor the warning button, and an inert declaration is exactly what the
lint is meant to catch. It changes nothing on screen.
