## Logic that must not be lost: why the pop-up's figures differ from the card

(Carried from the owner's Build Sheet, 08-10 Sep, so it survives the removal of history comments from the code. This is the logic Value Labs and the backend team need.)

**Two tiers, two pacing definitions, on purpose.** The card is the overview; the pop-up is where the detailed work happens.

| | Card, every size | Pledge pop-up |
|---|---|---|
| Pacing basis | **Term elapsed:** `expected = goal x (daysElapsed / termDays)` per pledge's own term, summed | **Instalments due:** for each pledge, the instalments whose due date has passed, from `BeginDate`, `Frequency`, `Duration` |
| Unit of "behind" | days | payments |
| Cost | one grouped read (as legacy) | per-pledge iteration, scoped to one activity |
| Answers | "is this activity roughly keeping up?" | "who has missed a payment, which one, how much is unfunded?" |

The card keeps term-elapsed because it is what legacy did, it is adequate for an overview and, decisively, **it is the only basis that scales**: a mega-church activity can carry thousands of pledgers, each with its own start and end date; building an instalment schedule for all of them to draw a KPI tile cannot be indexed or cached (it depends on the as-of date). Term-elapsed is a single `GROUP BY`. The pop-up is scoped to one activity, so its instalment work is bounded by that activity's pledge count.

**One exception crosses over (T10, 10 Sep):** the `Pledges behind` **count** on the card row is instalment-derived, so an activity that reads healthy overall still reveals the one or two pledges behind inside it. Every *money* figure on the row stays term-elapsed. Backend options for that count: materialise a missed-instalment count per pledge when receipts post (a plain `SUM`), or compute live below a row-count threshold.

**Known, accepted cost:** the activity row nets, so an activity can read "keeping up" while pledgers under it have missed payments. This is the masking the Step 5 aggregate rule (`SUM(MAX(0, expected - paid))`, never `MAX(0, SUM(expected) - SUM(paid))`) exists to prevent; accepted here because the alternative does not scale. Wording must read "the activity is keeping up overall, but these pledgers have missed payments", never as a defect.

**Which numbers tie out and which do not.** Goal, Paid, Outstanding and % Paid are definitional (receipts summed; goal minus receipts) and reconcile under either basis. **Exactly one figure diverges: expected-by-now**, and with it the status chip and the "behind" phrase. That is why the two are never shown side by side under the same name: **the label names the basis at both tiers** (the card says term pacing, the pop-up says instalments due), so a reader is told why the numbers differ instead of assuming one is wrong. This is also why some names and titles differ slightly between card and pop-up.

**What the pop-up is for.** Tapping an activity answers "what pledges sit behind this activity, and which are falling behind?", not "what money arrived recently". Jo's receipts list flattened payments and stripped the pledge and its term; the pledge list restores the level the widget is about: each pledge, its term, goal, paid, outstanding, pacing status, and its payment history.

**Route.** Her pacing bars and her table rows fire the same open action, so bar and row open one shared pop-up; ours keeps that single route.
