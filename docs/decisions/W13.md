## Why every earlier Kanban was wrong
Jo's board (live and phase 2) assigned its three post-approval lanes from `hash(id) % 3`, derived the number of approval steps from the amount and "steps done" from a status string, and let "next" orders be approved out of turn. Ours had real states (Pending / Approved / Rejected) but no notion of whose turn it was: approvers were a display list, anyone could drag anything, and Rejected was a lane. Both put the approver's turn on the status axis. In the system it lives on the approval path.

## The legacy model (MBAccounting, `POOrderRepository.cs`; see the reference memory for file pointers)
- One record status: Unapproved (0), Approved (1), Closed (2), Voided (3). **Rejected is never stored**: it is a rejected approval row on an Unapproved request. **Paid is never stored**: it is derived from the linked AP invoice's posted journal.
- Two paths per record: the Approval Path (request) and the Payment Approval Path (per invoice, after approval). A path is ordered levels: "Then" opens a new level, "Or" adds an interchangeable approver to the same level; each approver has a dollar threshold below which the level is skipped. Any one approver satisfies a level; approving at a higher level implies the lower ones (the server does not enforce order; the grid and the "awaiting next" query do).
- "Awaiting my approval next" = I am on the path and `Max(acted Sequence) + 1` is my level. "Awaiting my approval" = I am on the path and have not acted, wherever my level sits. Hold is a per-row reason (and a known legacy bug: it counts as acted, so the next level sees the request as theirs).

## What the rebuild does (ours)
- **Lanes are derived**, never the raw status: Pending approval (Unapproved) | Payment approval (Approved, payment path open) | Ready to pay (payment approved, no check) | Paid (check posted); Closed / Voided stay in the table and the Finish column. Selecting a lane on the status chip shows that lane alone.
- **Turn badge on every card**, from the live path: "Your approval next (level k of n)", "Your approval later. Waiting on X", "Waiting on A or B (level k of n, payment)", "Rejected by X: reason", "On hold: reason". Only a card the viewer can act on (their turn, Ready to pay, Paid) is draggable.
- **Scope chip** with the legacy vocabulary: Awaiting my approval next / Awaiting my approval / All requests. Headline = requests awaiting me; context line = pending count and outstanding; Glance = the same figure with Pending / Payment / Ready tiles.
- **A drop is an act on the live path**: Pending approval -> Payment approval records my approval (completing the request path when every applicable level is satisfied, which sets Approved); Payment approval -> Ready to pay records my payment approval; Ready to pay -> Paid enters the payment; Paid -> Close; unpaid -> Void. One lane at a time; refusals name the turn.
- **Approvals tab**: one row per level of the path ("Starts with / Then / Ends with A or B (from $N)"), Approved / Rejected / Hold boxes enabled on the viewer's level only, "Implied by a higher level" and "Skipped: below this level's threshold" states, updated-by per row; a second grid for the Payment Approval Path once Approved. A turn line at the top states whose approval is next.
- **Demo data** carries acted rows on both paths (`appr`, `payAppr`) with threshold and Or cases, rejected and held rows, and payment-approved and paid records.
