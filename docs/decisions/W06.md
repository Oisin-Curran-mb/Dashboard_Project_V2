## Logic that must not be lost
- **Nested table:** type rows are the parents (enrolled + cost subtotals), collapsed by default, an instant client toggle (no fetch) tracked in `insExpanded`; plan rows carry enrolled, Share of total (row enrolled / grand total in view) and Cost.
- **One fetch:** the insurance-type filter chip (800 ms skeleton). Sort, expand and the view segment are client re-renders.
- **Pie:** Table is the default at Explore (pie-as-default was never owner-confirmed; carried open); Detail shows both. Zero-enrolment plans are never charted; a note says how many are uncharted and why; a filter whose plans all have zero enrolment shows a chart-empty state naming the filter.
- **Cost definition:** total premium (SUM of each enrolment's Rate). Employer share (Rate minus RateIndividual) and employee share (RateIndividual) are derivable from the same data; PreTax and EmployerBilled are flags on the enrolment. Dependents carry their own premium and are counted.
- **Open items carried:** pie-as-default at Explore; the coverage-tier (IBTypeElection) third level intentionally not surfaced; the "total cost" definition is a product/SME choice.
